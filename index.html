The Story

After years as a glassblower, life has led me to laser engraving. Now I craft custom tiles and coasters, and other beautiful items, bringing old cool designs and clever ideas from South Carolina to life with precision and care.

Every piece starts with a design that means something—whether it’s the Palmetto tree and crescent, a Celtic knot that has traveled centuries, a quiet landscape, or a symbol that simply makes someone smile. I engrave on ceramic tile, slate, wood, leather-wrapped flasks, and more. Two laser engravers, steady hands, and a love for the craft.

A photograph fades. A tile does not. I can take your most important images and engrave them so they last and stand the test of time.
